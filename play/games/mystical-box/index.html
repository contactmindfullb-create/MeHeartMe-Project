MISSION 01

The Majestic Mirror Maze

At the edge of the Land of Shapes, the Majestic Mirror Palace has gone silent.

Its crystal corridors once reflected every color in the kingdom. Now the reflections move when no one is there, familiar doorways lead to impossible places, and shape citizens have begun disappearing behind the glass.

Olivia and Trevor arrive at the palace gates and discover two glowing paths. One is marked with a diamond. The other bears a shield.

Somewhere deep inside the maze, something is watching.

The twins must find the missing citizens, discover what is controlling the mirrors, and restore the Majestic Gateway before the palace seals itself forever.

YOUR DECISION

MISSION 01 · THE FINAL REFLECTION

The Secret Behind the Mirrors

The final puzzle clicks into place. Every mirror in the palace goes dark.

Then, in the largest mirror, a violet silhouette begins to move.

The shadow twists into a floating figure with a mischievous grin.

"You found me," the ghost whispers. "I am Mirra. I only wanted the world to see me."

Olivia and Trevor refuse to leave the trapped citizens behind. Together, they break the illusion and open the Majestic Gateway.

✨ Mission Complete! The shape citizens are free.

MISSION 02 · POLYGON KEEP

The Mystery of Polygon Keep

Polygon Keep was preparing for its grand medieval tournament when something extraordinary happened.

Empty armored boots began marching across the drawbridge. Shields spun through the air. The castle gates slammed shut.

Then a thunderous voice echoed through the Great Hall.

"BEHOLD THE GREATEST KNIGHT IN ALL THE KINGDOMS!"

Olivia and Trevor must rescue the trapped shape citizens and discover who is controlling the castle.

The Knight Who Found Courage

"BEHOLD MY TERRIFYING—"

CLINK!

"WAIT! WHAT'S THAT NOISE?!"

The enormous suit of armor rattles apart. Inside is Clank, a frightened ghost who dreams of becoming a legendary knight.

Suddenly, the enchanted castle gate begins falling toward the trapped citizens.

The citizens are saved. Polygon Keep is restored, and Clank becomes its newest guardian.

MISSION 02 · CINEMATIC EPILOGUE

The Hollow Design

As Polygon Keep grows quiet, something stirs inside the discarded armor. A tiny geometric assistant crawls from a hidden compartment. On its surface glimmers a familiar insignia: the ShapeScientist's mark. But this assistant is different. Deep inside its body is an empty chamber, just large enough for a ghost. Somewhere far away, a shadow watches. The mystery is only beginning.
